# The World Now

## Year: 2026

Thirty-three years post-Sundering. A generation has grown up knowing nothing else.

## Daily Reality

- **No nations remain** — authority is local. Factions control territory, trade routes, and resources.
- **Technology is 1993 or cruder** — existing pre-Sundering devices are treasured relics. Post-Sundering fabrication uses scrap and salvage.
- **No internet, no GPS, no cell phones** — communication is face-to-face, radio (where radios survive), or messenger. Maps are hand-drawn and traded like currency.
- **Thaumic contamination is ambient** — low-level supernatural phenomena are normal. Anomalies, reality distortions, and supernatural creatures are hazards like weather.
- **Religion dominates social organization** — Puritan factions filled the power vacuum with doctrine and Celestial-backed authority. Faith language carries real power.
- **Human population is a fraction of pre-Sundering** — scattered settlements, heavily fortified, always under threat.
- **The supernatural is real and terrifying** — not abstract belief but daily physical danger. Sanity is a resource that erodes with exposure.

## Geography

The game world is **deliberately unnamed and geographically ambiguous** — a composite post-industrial wasteland. No real-world country or region is specified.

Terrain types:
- Urban ruins (cities, suburbs, industrial parks)
- Wilderness (overgrown highways, collapsed bridges, feral terrain)
- Fortified settlements (walled compounds, bunker towns, trade posts)
- Infernal zones (corrupted overworld regions — flesh, sulfur, bone)
- Celestial zones (radiant overworld regions — light, stone, silence)
- Reality-warped pockets (non-Euclidean spaces near severe Veil damage)

## The Tech Cap

No technology exists beyond what was available in **1993**, because The Sundering destroyed global infrastructure and halted all industrial progress.

### What Exists
- **Pre-Sundering tech** (1993 and earlier): Firearms, vehicles (fuel-scarce), radios, basic electronics, medical equipment. All are aging, irreplaceable, and treasured.
- **Post-Sundering scrap-tech**: Crude fabrications from salvaged materials. Lower quality than pre-Sundering equivalents. Functional but ugly.
- **Thaumic-augmented devices**: Pre-Sundering equipment modified with thaumic components. Rare, valuable, unreliable, dangerous. Sometimes superior to conventional equivalents.

### What Does NOT Exist
- No post-1993 inventions (no smartphones, no modern internet, no drones, no LED screens)
- No mass manufacturing — everything is handmade, repaired, or scavenged
- No global logistics — trade is local, caravans between settlements
- No modern medicine beyond what's been preserved or improvised

### Cultural Implications
- Music, fashion, and cultural references are frozen at 1993 or earlier
- Younger generations have fragments of pre-Sundering culture passed down as oral history or surviving media (VHS tapes, cassettes, printed books)
- The pre-Sundering world is remembered with a mix of nostalgia, resentment, and mythologizing

## Hell/Heaven Zones

Some areas of the overworld have **fully collapsed** — the Veil is gone and the material world is replaced by supernatural reality.

### Infernal Zones
- Terrain is visibly corrupted: flesh walls, sulfur pools, bone architecture
- Temperature extremes — scorching heat or unnatural cold
- Demons are native here; humans are invaders
- Extremely dangerous but contains unique resources and lore fragments
- Expand slowly if unchecked; faction activity (esp. demon rituals) can accelerate this

### Celestial Zones
- Radiant ruins, petrified worshippers frozen in prayer, blinding light
- Beautiful and lethal — awe-inspiring but psychically overwhelming
- Angels manifest freely; broken angels are common hazards
- Sanity drains fast from sustained Celestial exposure
- Stable if left alone; Puritan factions treat them as holy sites

### Gameplay Implications
- Hell/heaven zones are visible on the overworld map — the player sees the corruption/radiance
- Entering is a deliberate choice, not an ambush
- Both zone types contain endgame-tier resources and narrative content
- Timing visits is a strategic decision (Veil fluctuations change zone difficulty)
