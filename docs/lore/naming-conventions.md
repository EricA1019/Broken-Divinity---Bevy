# Naming Conventions

Rules for naming entities, locations, items, and factions in Broken Divinity. Consistency here is critical — names carry worldbuilding weight and signal faction/species identity to the player.

## Species Naming

### Angelic Names
**Source language**: Hebrew-inspired

**Rules**:
- End in **-el**, **-iel**, **-ael**, or **-iah** (traditional angelic suffixes meaning "of God")
- 2-4 syllables preferred
- Soft consonants, flowing vowels
- Named angels (Gabriel, Michael, Raphael, Uriel) use traditional names — do not invent replacements
- Lesser angels may not have individual names — referred to by choir role (e.g., "the Shomer at the eastern gate")

**Acceptable examples**: Sariel, Azrael, Cassiel, Nuriel, Zaphkiel, Hanael, Barachiel
**Unacceptable**: Demonyx, Krath'iel, Bladeangel (too fantasy, too edgy, too game-named)

**Angelic titles and terms** (used in speech and lore text):
| Term | Meaning | Usage |
|------|---------|-------|
| Adonai | The divine authority | Referenced by angels, never directly encountered |
| Adam / B'nei Adam | Humans | Classification, not derogatory |
| B'nei Elohim | Angels ("children of God") | Self-reference |
| Shomrim | Guardians | Common protocol role |
| Malakhim | Messengers | Common protocol role |
| Ofanim | Wheels (a choir type) | Higher-rank reference |

---

### Demonic Names
**Source language**: Akkadian/Sumerian-inspired

**Rules**:
- Harder consonants: **k**, **z**, **sh**, **th**, **g**, **r**
- Often include **-ur**, **-al**, **-esh**, **-tu**, **-ki**, **-gal** suffixes
- 2-4 syllables preferred
- Can include glottal stops or apostrophes sparingly (Ish'tar, not Ish'tar'kek'thul)
- Named archfiends use Mesopotamian mythological names or derivations

**Acceptable examples**: Nergal, Ereshkigal, Pazuzu, Lamashtu, Namtar, Gallu, Asag, Utukku
**Unacceptable**: Demonlord, Xx_Shadowfiend, Malachar (too generic fantasy)

**Demonic titles and terms**:
| Term | Meaning | Usage |
|------|---------|-------|
| Gal | Great / chief | Title prefix for powerful demons |
| Ensi | Governor / ruler | Mid-rank title |
| Lugal | King | Archfiend title |
| Irkalla | The underworld | The Court's domain reference |
| Kur | The land below | Alternative underworld reference |
| Udug / Utukku | Spirit / demon | Generic classifier |

---

### Human Names
**Source**: No single cultural source. Post-Sundering humans are a cultural blend.

**Rules**:
- Use common, grounded names — avoid fantasy naming
- Faction members may adopt names from their faction's naming culture (a Puritan might take a Hebrew-adjacent name; an Occultist might use a Latin-adjacent one)
- Survivors use whatever names they were born with
- Nicknames and callsigns are common (especially Military archetype)

## Faction Naming

### Hardcoded Faction Names
These are fixed and must not change:

| Faction | Name | Reasoning |
|---------|------|-----------|
| Survivor | *(Unnamed — it's the player's settlement)* | Player names their own shelter |
| Puritan | Michael's Host | Named for their leader, Archangel Michael |
| Infernal | The Court of Irkalla | Sumerian underworld — fits demon naming pattern |
| Occultist | The Lethean Circle | Greek (Lethe = forgetting) — academic/esoteric tone |
| Military | Fort Pershing Garrison | Named for location, military convention |

### Proc-Gen Faction Names

Generated by combining elements from the faction's **archetype word pool**:

**Puritan pattern**: [Religious title/term] + [of/from] + [Place or Virtue]
- *The Order of the Sealed Gate*
- *The Congregation of Burning Light*
- *Shepherds of the New Covenant*

**Infernal pattern**: [Court/Horde/Pack/Legion] + [of] + [Sumerian/Akkadian term or concept]
- *The Horde of Namtar*
- *Legion of the Broken Gate*
- *The Pack of Gallu*

**Occultist pattern**: [Greek/Latin academic term] + [Circle/Society/Institute/Cabal]
- *The Hermetic Society*
- *The Athanor Cabal*
- *Prometheus Institute*

**Military pattern**: [Location/Landmark] + [military unit]
- *Highway 9 Company*
- *The Dam Patrol*
- *Ridgeline Militia*

**Survivor pattern**: [Descriptive location or trait] + [community word]
- *Bridgewater Settlement*
- *The Free Traders*
- *Ashfield Commune*

## Location Naming

### Overworld Nodes
Named descriptively based on what they are:
- Settlements: Named by inhabitants (player names their own)
- Dungeon entrances: Descriptive + theme hint (*The Sunken Clinic*, *Sector 7 Labs*, *The Cathedral of Silence*)
- Natural features: Grounded, geographic (*Miller's Creek*, *The Eastern Rise*, *Dead Highway*)
- Faction territories: Named by controlling faction

### Dungeon Rooms
Internal room names (shown in UI) should be short and evocative:
- Good: *Lobby*, *Cold Storage*, *The Altar Room*, *Containment Bay 3*, *The Pit*
- Bad: *Room 47*, *Unnamed Chamber*, *Dark Scary Room* (too generic or too on-the-nose)

## Item Naming

### Weapons and Armor
**Pattern**: [Material/Quality] + [Type]
- Common: *Scrap Pipe*, *Makeshift Shield*, *Salvaged Rifle*
- Quality: *Reinforced Steel Blade*, *Woven Kevlar Vest*
- Thaumic: *Conduit-Wired Blade*, *Resonance Plating*
- Faction: Named items carry faction naming conventions — a Puritan weapon might be *Michael's Judgment*, an Infernal one might be *Nergal's Claw*

### Consumables
Grounded, practical names: *Canned Rations*, *Medical Kit*, *Rift Stabilizer Charge*, *Water Purification Tablet*

### Lore Items
The most evocative names in the game. These should make the player want to read the description:
- *Fragment of the Weeping Broadcast*
- *Terminal Entry: Project THRESHOLD*
- *Recovered Journal — Entry 47*
- *Containment Log: Subject Aleph*

---

## What to Avoid

Across all naming:
- No apostrophe soup (*Kra'th'zul'ek*)
- No portmanteau edginess (*Deathlord*, *Shadowbane*, *Hellknight*)
- No fourth-wall breaking (*Player's Sword*, *Tutorial Key*)
- No inconsistency within a species (an angel named "Bloodfang")
- No modern slang from characters who shouldn't use it (a 500-year-old angel saying "bruh")
