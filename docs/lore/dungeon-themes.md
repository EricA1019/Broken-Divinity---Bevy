# Dungeon Themes

All dungeons are BSP-generated (Binary Space Partitioning) — rooms connected by corridors. Theme determines visual flavor, hazard types, enemy roster, loot pools, and ambient sanity pressure. A dungeon's theme is set by its overworld location and whatever faction or phenomenon controls the site.

## The 11 Themes

### Urban Decay

**Setting**: Rusted cars, collapsed apartment blocks, feral animals, squatter camps. Pre-Sundering billboards and signs peeling.

**Primary hazards**: Structural collapse, territorial scavengers, ambush-prone sightlines.

**Enemy flavor**: Human raiders, feral animals, scavenger gangs. Mundane — the danger is numbers and desperation.

**Loot flavor**: Pre-Sundering canned goods, scrap metal, salvageable electronics, clothing. Occasionally a hidden stash with real value.

**Sanity pressure**: Low. This is the most "normal" environment. Sanity threats come from violence, not the supernatural.

---

### Religious

**Setting**: Defiled cathedrals, scripture carved into walls, melted candles in vast halls. Stained glass casting wrong-colored light. Pews overturned or arranged into barricades.

**Primary hazards**: Celestial residue OR Infernal corruption, depending on which faction holds the site. Contested sites have both.

**Enemy flavor**: Puritans (fanatical human defenders), angelic sentinels (lesser angels holding protocol positions), or demonic occupiers. The worst sites have all three fighting each other.

**Loot flavor**: Religious texts (some containing lore fragments), ceremonial weapons, thaumic-reactive relics, anointed armor.

**Sanity pressure**: Moderate-High. The divine/infernal residue creates constant low-level pressure even in empty rooms.

---

### Underground

**Setting**: Collapsed metro tunnels, fungal growth, dripping water, maintenance tunnel networks. Darkness pressing in from all directions.

**Primary hazards**: Darkness (limited visibility), unstable passages, flooding, cave-ins. Navigational hazards — getting lost is a real threat.

**Enemy flavor**: Subterranean creatures, tunnel-dwelling factions, Veil-mutated vermin. Things that thrive where the surface world doesn't reach.

**Loot flavor**: Pre-Sundering infrastructure supplies, metro maps (navigation value), preserved equipment sealed in waterproof containers.

**Sanity pressure**: Low-Moderate. Darkness and isolation erode steadily rather than spiking.

---

### Corporate

**Setting**: Frozen-in-1993 office towers. Cubicles, fax machines, filing cabinets. Eerily preserved — coffee cups still on desks, papers mid-sentence. Fluorescent lights hum intermittently.

**Primary hazards**: Temporal anomalies (time loops, echo events), psychic residue of mass death, structural instability from 33 years of neglect masked by the preservation effect.

**Enemy flavor**: Psychic echoes (hostile manifestations of death events), anomalous entities drawn to temporal distortion, scavengers who underestimated the site.

**Loot flavor**: Information — pre-Sundering corporate records, government contracts, research memos. Often the most narratively valuable dungeon type.

**Sanity pressure**: Moderate. The preservation effect is inherently unsettling. Temporal anomalies spike exposure hard.

---

### Medical

**Setting**: Abandoned hospitals, failed thaumic experiments, specimen jars, surgery theaters. The border between medicine and thaumaturgy blurred here. Sterile white gone yellow with age.

**Primary hazards**: Mutated subjects (failed experiments still alive), contaminated zones, unstable thaumic equipment, biohazard.

**Enemy flavor**: Mutated humans and animals, rogue thaumic constructs, desperate survivors using the site as shelter. The worst sites contain subjects that were deliberately modified.

**Loot flavor**: Medical supplies (always valuable), thaumic research notes, pharmaceutical equipment, experimental thaumic devices (powerful but unstable).

**Sanity pressure**: Moderate-High. The evidence of what was done here — and what it produced — erodes steadily.

---

### Industrial

**Setting**: Factories, foundries, assembly lines. Some repurposed as Military fortifications or Occultist workshops. Heavy machinery, toxic atmosphere, catwalks and vertical spaces.

**Primary hazards**: Industrial machinery (some still running), toxic atmosphere, structural collapse, fire/explosions from volatile materials.

**Enemy flavor**: Faction troops using the site as a base, automated defense systems, creatures nesting in the superstructure.

**Loot flavor**: Raw materials (metal, chemicals, fuel), repurposable machinery components, weapons forged on-site.

**Sanity pressure**: Low. Industrial sites are dangerous but not inherently supernatural. Sanity threats come from whatever moved in, not the site itself.

---

### Scientific

**Setting**: Thaumic laboratories, rift monitoring equipment, containment chambers. Occultist territory. Whiteboards covered in equations. Equipment still humming, still drawing power from unknown sources.

**Primary hazards**: Reality instability (physics may not behave normally), thaumic contamination zones, containment breaches, unstable rift points.

**Enemy flavor**: Escaped containment subjects, rogue thaumic phenomena, Occultist researchers who went too deep, entities that came through research rifts.

**Loot flavor**: Thaumic equipment (highest-tier science loot), research data (lore-critical), containment devices, rare materials.

**Sanity pressure**: High. Active thaumic equipment saturates the area. Reality instability is a direct sanity attack.

---

### Military

**Setting**: Bunkers, armories, command posts, barracks. Disciplined layout, defensive positions, supply caches. Pre-Sundering military infrastructure.

**Primary hazards**: Defensive systems (automated turrets, mines, locked blast doors), fortified enemy positions, booby traps left by previous occupants.

**Enemy flavor**: Fort Pershing Garrison patrols, rival faction troops, scavenger gangs using military equipment. Occupied sites are organized; abandoned sites are trapped.

**Loot flavor**: Weapons, ammunition, body armor, tactical equipment. The most reliable source of conventional military gear.

**Sanity pressure**: Low. Military sites are mundane. Sanity threats only arise if something supernatural moved in — which, 33 years post-Sundering, it often has.

---

### Infernal

**Setting**: Terrain itself is corrupted. Flesh walls, sulfur pools, bone architecture. Temperature extremes — searing heat in open areas, freezing cold in deep chambers. The Veil is thin or absent.

**Primary hazards**: Environmental damage (heat, acid, toxic air), demonic entities in their native environment, corruption spreading to equipment and characters.

**Enemy flavor**: Demons of all tiers in their home territory. They are stronger here. The environment itself attacks — walls shift, floors give way, passages seal behind you.

**Loot flavor**: Infernal materials (used in some thaumic applications), corrupted relics (powerful but sanity-costly), demon trophies, rare biological samples.

**Sanity pressure**: Very High. The infernal environment is a constant assault — temptation, paranoia, rage. Raid Exposure accumulates faster than any other theme.

**Feel**: Deep crimson, sickly orange, wet organic sounds, sulfur and copper blood. See [species.md](species.md) for the full infernal sensory table.

---

### Celestial

**Setting**: Radiant ruins, petrified worshippers frozen in prayer, blinding light in sealed chambers. Geometric perfection in architecture — too precise, too symmetrical. Beautiful and lethal.

**Primary hazards**: Celestial exposure (sanity drain from divine presence), angelic sentinels enforcing ancient protocols, light-based attacks, compulsion effects.

**Enemy flavor**: Angels holding positions they've held for 33 years. Not hostile by choice — hostile by protocol. "This area is restricted. Unauthorized entities will be purified." Lesser angels are mechanical. Greater angels may negotiate.

**Loot flavor**: Celestial materials (used in some thaumic applications), divine relics (powerful but sanity-costly), angelic equipment (protocol-locked — may require negotiation or force to claim).

**Sanity pressure**: Very High. The celestial environment attacks through awe and compulsion. You don't want to leave. You want to kneel. You want to stay.

**Feel**: Cold gold, white radiance, harmonic drone, ozone and metallic absence. See [species.md](species.md) for the full angelic sensory table.

---

### Reality-Warped

**Setting**: Non-Euclidean spaces, temporal echoes, gravity shifts. The Veil is not thin here — it is **torn**. No faction controls these sites. They form around severe Veil damage and change shape over time.

**Primary hazards**: Physics failure (gravity shifts, spatial loops, time distortion), entities from deep past the Veil (neither cleanly angelic nor demonic), navigation itself becomes a puzzle.

**Enemy flavor**: Anomalous entities that defy classification. Things from deep past the Veil that don't map to angel/demon taxonomy. Remnants of whatever was here before the rupture — warped, recycled, wrong.

**Loot flavor**: Anomalous materials (unique properties, unpredictable), Veil fragments (critical for narrative progression), reality-anchoring devices, pre-Sundering artifacts warped into new forms.

**Sanity pressure**: Extreme. The most dangerous theme. The environment is an active attack on human perception. Short runs or lose the character.

**Feel**: All bets off. Colors shift. Geometry lies. Sound arrives before its source. The map changes between visits.

---

## Theme Mixing

Dungeons are primarily one theme, but **zones within a dungeon can shift**:
- An Urban Decay building with an Infernal-corrupted basement
- A Military bunker with a Scientific lab wing
- A Religious site where one wing has gone Celestial and another Infernal

Theme transitions are signaled visually and through ambient sanity pressure changes, giving the player warning before they cross a threshold.
