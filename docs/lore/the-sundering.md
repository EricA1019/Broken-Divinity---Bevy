# The Sundering

## What Happened

**Date**: 1993

The boundary between the material world and the supernatural realms — called the **Veil** — catastrophically ruptured. The cause is debated:

- **Puritan doctrine** claims humanity's sin invited divine judgment
- **Occultists** believe a grand thaumic experiment destabilized the Veil
- The truth may be both, neither, or something else entirely — this is the central mystery of the game

## Immediate Effects

- Celestial and infernal entities physically entered the material world
- Global infrastructure collapsed — power grids, communications, supply chains, manufacturing
- Thaumic energy saturated the environment, warping terrain and biology
- Mass psychic trauma — millions experienced visions, madness, or death from exposure
- Technology development froze at its 1993 state; no new manufacturing capacity emerged

## The Veil Today

The Veil is not sealed, not fully open. It is a wound that seeps.

- **Thin spots**: Locations where entities cross more easily. Dungeons often form around these weak points.
- **Collapsed zones**: Some areas have fully given way — the material world is replaced by infernal or celestial reality. These are permanent overworld zones visible on the map.
- **Fluctuation**: The Veil is not static. Some thin spots close, new ones open. Occultist experiments and demon rituals can widen them; other forces work to narrow them.
- **Ambient thaumic energy**: Post-Sundering, low-level supernatural phenomena are normal background hazards — like weather.

## The Mystery

The Sundering is the central narrative mystery. Discovering its true cause is the deepest reward in the game.

### What Players Can Learn

The truth is **layered and faction-gated**:

1. **Surface knowledge** (common) — "The Veil broke in 1993. Angels and demons appeared. The world ended."
2. **Faction narratives** (reputation-gated) — Each faction has an explanation. Puritans blame human sin. Occultists blame a specific experiment. The Military blames the Occultists. Demons claim credit. Angels say nothing useful.
3. **Deep fragments** (dungeon exploration) — Journals, inscriptions, pre-Sundering terminals, anomaly readings. These predate or contradict faction narratives.
4. **The truth** (endgame) — Requires synthesizing deep fragments with information from multiple factions. No single faction has the complete picture. Some are lying. Some are wrong. Some don't know what they know.

### What We Know (Design-Level)

The actual cause of The Sundering is a design-level secret. Key constraints:

- It must implicate at least two factions (preventing a simple villain)
- It must explain why both angels and demons emerged simultaneously
- It must leave room for the player to choose who to believe
- It must be discoverable but never forced — the sandbox path (ignore the narrative, just survive) is always valid
