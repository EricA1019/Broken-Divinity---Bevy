# Species

Three species inhabit the post-Sundering world. Species affects starting stats, available backgrounds, faction disposition, NPC reactions, and sanity interactions.

## Human

The default. Humans are the most numerous, most adaptable, and most diverse species. They built the pre-Sundering world and now scavenge its ruins.

### Traits
- **Stat baseline**: Balanced, no extreme strengths or weaknesses
- **Thaumaturgy**: The only species that practices it — angels and demons channel power innately, humans need equipment
- **Faction access**: Welcome (or at least tolerated) by all archetypes except hard-line Puritans (suspicious) and Infernal (contemptuous)
- **Sanity**: Standard dual-track (raid exposure + long-term erosion)

### Backgrounds
Humans have the widest background selection: Founder, Exile, Scavenger, Deserter, and others TBD.

## Angelic

Beings from the other side of the Veil — the "divine" side. They are not gods. They are not good. They are **protocol-driven entities** displaced by a catastrophe they don't fully understand.

### Identity
- **Pronouns**: They/them (nonbinary)
- **Presentation**: Feminine-presenting across all ranks
- **Names**: Hebrew-inspired (see [naming-conventions.md](naming-conventions.md))
- **Age**: Effectively immortal — existed before The Sundering, most are centuries or millennia old
- **Reproduction**: Unknown. No observed angelic children or creation events post-Sundering

### Nature
Angels are **near-automatons**. Each was created with a complex set of protocols — duties, hierarchies, rituals, responses — that governed their existence behind the Veil. Post-Sundering, most of these protocols are **useless**. The chain of command is broken. The purpose they served no longer exists. They keep performing fragments of protocol anyway, because they have nothing else.

Key behavioral traits:
- **Lack imagination**: Cannot easily adapt to new situations. Lower-rank angels are nearly mechanical in this regard.
- **Higher rank = more creative**: Archangels (Gabriel, Michael) can reason, improvise, and form genuine relationships. Lesser angels repeat routines.
- **Protocol adherence**: Even when a protocol is meaningless, they perform it. An angel assigned to "guard the eastern gate" will stand at coordinates that no longer correspond to anything.
- **Emotional range**: Present but constrained. They feel, but express through protocol. Grief looks like repetitive ritual. Joy looks like fulfilled duty.

### Appearance

#### Sane
- Beautiful in a way that is **too perfect** — unsettling, not attractive
- Flowing hair, range of skin tones, faint luminescence
- Gold or white eyes, no visible pupil
- Proportions slightly off — too symmetrical, too smooth, limbs fractionally too long
- Dressed in layered vestments that seem to move independently of wind

#### Broken
The **glitch aesthetic**. When an angel's sanity breaks, the divine mask drops and what's underneath is not flesh — it's something the human mind can't process:
- Reality stutters in their vicinity — visual static, flickering geometry
- Sound distortion — their voice becomes layered harmonics, ear-splitting
- Physical form flickers between states — solid, translucent, impossible
- Surroundings warp — straight lines bend, light refracts wrong
- They don't become monstrous (that's demons). They become **incomprehensible**.

### Communication

Rank-dependent:

| Rank | Speech Pattern | Example |
|------|---------------|---------|
| Lesser | Harmonic drone, single repeated phrases | *"The gate. The gate. The eastern gate."* |
| Greater | Stilted, formal, protocol-laden | *"You are not of the Host. State your designation or be classified as intruder."* |
| Archangel | Fluent, archaic-formal, occasionally warm | *"I remember the gate. It meant something, once. I think it meant safety."* |

Hebrew terms are used throughout angelic speech:
- **Adonai**: The divine authority (absent since The Sundering)
- **Adam**: Humans (not derogatory — classificatory)
- **B'nei Elohim**: Angels (literally "children of God")
- **Shomrim**: Guardians (a common protocol role)

### Hierarchy

Traditional 9 choirs exist in lore but collapse to **3 gameplay tiers**:

| Tier | Choirs (Lore) | Gameplay Role | Creativity |
|------|--------------|---------------|------------|
| Lesser | Angels, Archangels (lowercase), Principalities | Fodder, patrol, routine tasks | Near-zero |
| Greater | Powers, Virtues, Dominions | Officers, specialists, zone guardians | Low-moderate |
| Archangel | Thrones, Cherubim, Seraphim + named Archangels | Bosses, companions, quest-givers | High |

> Note: The "Archangels" choir in traditional hierarchy is *lowercase* — rank-and-file messengers. Gabriel and Michael are *uppercase* Archangels, which is a title of authority, not a choir membership.

### Relationship to Thaumaturgy
Angels do not use thaumaturgy. Their abilities are innate — channeled through their form, not through equipment. They view thaumaturgy with confusion (lesser), suspicion (greater), or cautious interest (archangel).

## Demonic

Beings from the other side of the Veil — the "corrupted" side. They are not evil by nature. They are **ambitious, adaptive, hierarchical beings** who see the post-Sundering world as territory.

### Identity
- **Pronouns**: Gendered like humans (he/him, she/her, they/them by individual)
- **Presentation**: Varies wildly — masculine, feminine, androgynous, inhuman
- **Names**: Akkadian/Sumerian-inspired (see [naming-conventions.md](naming-conventions.md))
- **Age**: Varies. Some are ancient. Some formed from Veil energy post-Sundering.
- **Reproduction**: Can reproduce. Demon offspring inherit traits. Half-demons (nephilim-equivalent) exist in lore but are rare.

### Nature
Demons are what angels are not: **adaptable, individualistic, ambitious**. Where angels cling to broken protocol, demons thrive in chaos. Their hierarchy is based on **power, not protocol** — the strongest lead, the weak serve or die.

Key behavioral traits:
- **Adaptable**: Quickly learn human language, customs, and manipulation
- **Ambitious**: Always seeking more power, territory, influence
- **Individualistic**: No shared protocol. Each demon has personal goals.
- **Emotional range**: Full and unrestrained. Rage, lust, grief, joy, cruelty, tenderness — all genuine, all weaponized.

### Appearance

Three broad categories (a spectrum, not discrete classes):

#### Traditional
- Horns, claws, tails, wings — the classic demonic form
- Often larger than human, sometimes much larger
- Obvious — no one mistakes them for human
- Common among older, more powerful demons who see no need to hide

#### Near-Human
- Appear almost entirely human at first glance
- Subtle tells: wrong eye color, faint sulfur scent, shadow that moves independently, teeth slightly too sharp
- Preferred by infiltrators, diplomats, and demons who live among humans
- Can suppress tells with effort, but never perfectly

#### Ancient/Monstrous
- **Mesopotamian mythical creatures**: lamassu (winged human-headed bulls), winged bulls, chimeric forms
- Massive, awe-inspiring, terrifying
- The oldest and most powerful demons, rarely seen
- Their forms predate human concepts of "demonic" — they're not evil-looking, they're *alien*-looking

### Communication

Type-dependent (correlates loosely with appearance):

| Type | Speech Pattern | Example |
|------|---------------|---------|
| Bestial | Snarls, roars, single words | *Growling* "MINE." |
| Broken | Fragmented, menacing, predatory | *"You… have something. Give. Or I take."* |
| Eloquent | Fluent, persuasive, dangerously charming | *"I could simply kill you. But where's the artistry in that? Let me propose an alternative."* |
| Telepathic | Intrusive, violating, unavoidable | *The thought arrives without permission: you should open the gate.* |

### Hierarchy

Power-based, not role-based:

| Tier | Description | Gameplay Role |
|------|-------------|---------------|
| Lesser | Newly formed, weak, often bestial | Fodder, dungeon trash |
| Greater | Established, territorial, cunning | Mid-bosses, faction leaders, quest-givers |
| Archfiend | Ancient, immensely powerful, often monstrous form | Major bosses, world-shaping NPCs |

### Relationship to Thaumaturgy
Demons do not use thaumaturgy — their abilities are innate, channeled through their form. However, unlike angels, demons are **interested** in thaumaturgy. Some Greater demons study it, trade for thaumic equipment, or manipulate human thaumaturges. The Court of Irkalla actively collects thaumic research.

## Angelic vs Infernal — Sensory Comparison

| Aspect | Angelic | Infernal |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Core horror | Perfection that cannot adapt | Corruption that adapts too well |
| Visual | Cold gold, white, glitch static | Deep crimson, sickly orange, flesh |
| Sound | Harmonic drone, tinnitus, silence | Wet sounds, crackling fire, whispers |
| Smell | Ozone, metallic, absence | Sulfur, copper blood, rot |
| Architecture | Geometric perfection, radiant stone | Organic growth, bone, meat |
| Broken form | Reality glitch — incomprehensible | Body horror — fusion, mutation |
| Sanity attack | Awe / paralysis / compulsion | Temptation / paranoia / rage |
| Goal | Restore pre-Sundering Veil (old order) | Widen the Veil permanently |
| Why scary | Don't understand why you resist | Understand exactly why you resist |
