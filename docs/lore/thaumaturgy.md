# Thaumaturgy

## What It Is

A hybrid discipline born in the 18th-19th centuries when natural philosophers discovered that certain scientific instruments could interact with supernatural forces. **Not magic in the fantasy sense** — it's closer to engineering with anomalous materials.

The word "thaumaturgy" literally means "miracle-working," but practitioners treat it as applied science, not sorcery.

## How It Works

### Equipment Required
Thaumaturgy **cannot be performed bare-handed**. It requires:

- **Rift stabilizers** — devices that maintain a controlled connection to thaumic energy
- **Focusing lenses** — precision optics that direct thaumic energy
- **Conduit arrays** — circuits of treated materials that channel energy from source to effect
- **Resonance chambers** — contained environments for larger-scale operations

Equipment ranges from handheld (a focusing lens with conduit wiring) to room-sized (a full resonance chamber with stabilized rift).

### Training Required
Years of study in:
- Thaumic theory (how energy behaves)
- Material science (which materials channel, resist, or amplify thaumic energy)
- Safety protocols (how to avoid catastrophic equipment failure)
- Field engineering (how to improvise in hostile environments)

### Energy Source
Powered by **ambient thaumic energy** — the residual supernatural radiation that saturates the post-Sundering world. Pre-Sundering, this energy was scarce and required sophisticated equipment to access. Post-Sundering, it's plentiful but unstable.

Think of it as radiation: it's everywhere, it's dangerous in concentration, and it can be harnessed with the right equipment.

## History

### Pre-Sundering
- 18th-19th century: Natural philosophers discover thaumic interactions during early electrical experiments
- Thaumaturgy develops as a **secret academic discipline** — known to universities, governments, and military research, but not to the general public
- Mainstream technology develops normally alongside it
- Thaumic applications remain niche due to low ambient energy levels and expensive equipment

### Post-Sundering
- Ambient thaumic energy spikes massively — the discipline suddenly becomes viable at scale
- Most thaumic researchers are killed in The Sundering itself (universities, labs, and cities were primary targets of the Veil rupture)
- Surviving knowledge is fragmented and scattered
- Occultist factions form around recovered research, apprenticeship chains, and rediscovered equipment
- Puritan factions declare thaumaturgy heresy — driving it underground

## Danger

Thaumaturgy is **genuinely dangerous**, not just narrative window dressing:

- **Equipment overload**: Conduit arrays can melt, lenses crack, stabilizers fail. When they fail, they release uncontrolled thaumic energy.
- **Rift widening**: Badly calibrated operations can widen thin spots in the Veil, potentially opening new entry points for entities.
- **Thaumic contamination**: Practitioners exposed to sustained thaumic energy suffer sanity loss, and at extreme levels, physical mutation.
- **Unpredictability**: Post-Sundering ambient levels fluctuate. A safe operation one day can become catastrophic the next if a surge occurs.

## Thaumic Damage Type

In gameplay, thaumic damage is one of three supernatural damage types:

| Type | Source | Resisted By | Visual |
|------|--------|-------------|--------|
| Celestial | Angelic / Puritan | MD (Magic Defense) | Cold pale gold, white glow |
| Infernal | Demonic / Infernal | MD | Deep crimson, sickly orange |
| **Thaumic** | **Human thaumaturgy / Occultist** | **MD** | **Electric blue, unstable violet** |

Thaumic damage represents raw supernatural energy channeled through human engineering. It is distinct from Celestial (divine source) and Infernal (corrupted source).

## Relationship to the Veil

Thaumaturgy interacts directly with the Veil:
- Operations near thin spots are more powerful but riskier
- Stabilized rifts (like The Lethean Circle's) are controlled weak points used as permanent energy sources
- Thaumic equipment can detect Veil thickness — Occultists use this for navigation and prospecting
- Some theorize that thaumic experimentation contributed to The Sundering — this is the Occultists' greatest shame and the Puritans' primary accusation

## Language

When describing thaumaturgy, use **science-adjacent language**, not fantasy vocabulary:

| Use | Don't Use |
|-----|-----------|
| "Calibrate the conduit array" | "Channel the arcane energies" |
| "Rift stabilizer malfunction" | "The spell went wrong" |
| "Thaumic energy levels are spiking" | "The magical power grows" |
| "Conduit fatigue" | "Mana depletion" |
| "Field-expedient resonance setup" | "Improvised ritual" |
